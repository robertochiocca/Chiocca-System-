# Study 2 · Ground truth part 1 · Search record

Written by Roberto Chiocca with Claude (claude.ai), outside the agent's environment, without the alert list and without running the code, as section 5 of the study 2 pre-registration requires (commit `79c9214714696693235ce372b82cdaa1ad73c38e`). The boundary and qualification rules used here are those of amendment 1. The Portuguese version is the reference.

## Result

**No qualifying case.** By the freeze, no case meets section 5 with an affected period that includes 2024 or 2025. The file `data/gabarito_estudo_2_parte_1.csv` holds only the header. This is in line with expectations: the pre-registration estimates about 1.6 cases through 2029-12-31, and sanctioning proceedings about the content of financial statements usually take years to be opened.

## How the search was done

- **When:** from 2026-11-15 until the freeze.
- **Section 5 sources:**
  1. CVM sanctioning proceedings and Board (Colegiado) decisions;
  2. companies' material fact notices and market notices;
  3. the CVM list of restatement and republication orders (2025 and 2026 folders), checked by Roberto in a browser;
  4. for financial companies, sourced news about the Central Bank.
- **Search terms:** restatement order (refazimento), republication, restatement (reapresentação), error correction, CPC 23, accounting fraud, accounting inconsistencies, independent committee, sanctioning proceeding, and 2024 and 2025 financial statements.
- **Limit:** the search did not go through every 2025 and 2026 material fact notice one by one. A case that slipped through enters part 2, flagged as "not found in part 1" (amendment 1).

## May enter part 2

Grupo Mateus, Aegea, BRB and Ambipar. Being on this list is not an accusation and does not indicate an alert: it only records public facts that, at the freeze, do not meet section 5. No alert of theirs is described or published because they are here (amendment 1, item 5). If a document that qualifies one of them comes out after the freeze, the case enters part 2, and `data_publica` stays the first documented public date (amendment 1, item 1). For all four, that date is before 2026-04-30, so only the 2024 DFP can count (section 4.1).
