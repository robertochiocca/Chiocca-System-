# Engine card

Generated by `python -m chiocca.v2.ficha_do_motor` from `docs/resultados_numeros.json` (the same JSON as the [summary](../summary.md)) and the [descriptive analysis after the result](post-study-1-analyses.md). Do not edit by hand: a test compares this file with the script output. Model card format.

> Statistical signal based on public financial statements. Not an accusation, not investment advice.

## Version

- Engine: motor v1 (estudo 1), branch estudo-1-final, commit 8c9a823. Study 1 is frozen at that commit; the product uses the engine as it is.
- Product layer: chiocca.v2 (produto, fases B a E) (context, confidence, lineage, investigation, API and interface), without changing any engine value.
- Pre-registration signed on 2026-11-04, with 11 amendments ([`docs/pre-registro.md`](../pre-registro.md)).

## Intended use

- Point to where to look in the public financial statements of Brazilian listed companies, for human analysis.
- Each signal comes with the rule, the context (percentile in the sector and year, the company's own history), the confidence in the data and the lineage down to the document line.

## Out of scope

- Investment decisions.
- Accusation: an alert is a statistical signal, not proof of wrongdoing.
- Automated decisions about people or companies.

## Data

- CVM standardized financial statements (DFP), from the open data and, for old versions, from ENET, read "as they were": on each date, only what had been filed by then.
- Fiscal years 2010 to 2023; 2024 onward are sealed and reserved for study 2.
- Study 1 sample: 6,938 non-financial and 517 financial company-years; ground truth of 42 public cases (CVM conviction or admitted fraud), 13 of them detectable.

## Evaluation (study 1)

- Detections: 7 of 13 detectable cases (the naive rule detected 6); in the test window, 2 (naive rule: 1).
- False alarms per detected case, non-financial: 108.3 over the whole period and 311.0 in the test window.
- Alert rate: 49.9% of evaluated non-financial company-years (48.3% including financial ones).
- Test against chance (descriptive analysis after the result, not pre-registered): without assuming independent years, chance would give 7.37 detections, and 7 or more with probability 69.3%; assuming independent years, 8.56 detections (90.0%). The number of detections did not beat chance.
- Lead time of the detections (same nature): median of 19.4 months between the filing of the first DFP in alert in the affected period and the case's public date, from 3.4 to 46.9 months.

## Limitations

The same as in the README's "Limitations" section:

- The implied interest rate alone accounts for 59.8% of non-financial company-years in alert (2,001 of 3,345): finance costs (3.06.02) include exchange differences, lease interest and other items that are not interest on borrowings ([summary](../summary.md)).
- The M-score had no value in 1,548 of 6,938 company-years; in 1,306, DEPI was missing. The most common reason is a cash flow statement with no depreciation line in section 6.01.01 (1,135 company-years); when depreciation is combined with amortization in the same line, the total is used and DEPI is computed ([results](results.md)).
- The M-score cut-off of -1.78 comes from Beneish (1999), estimated with US companies, and was not recalibrated for Brazil ([summary](../summary.md)).
- IRB cannot be detected with annual DFPs (amendment 3); catching it would require quarterly data (ITR) ([summary](../summary.md)).
- Few cases: 13 detectable out of 42 in the ground truth, none of them financial ([summary](../summary.md)).
- The number of detections did not beat chance in the descriptive test done after the result: 7 detections, against 7.37 expected without assuming independent years (8.56 assuming independent years) ([analysis](post-study-1-analyses.md)).
- Old versions recovered from ENET exist only for consolidated statements: from 2010 to 2023, 190 company-years whose point-in-time version had individual statements only were left out (amendment 8) ([temporal storage](temporal-storage.md)).
- After the result, the data checks found 57 company-years in which an unfilled balance sheet entered the calculation as zero; 29 alerts depend on it. No ground-truth case was affected. Details in the [later note](post-study-1-analyses.md), section 4.

## Ethical considerations

- Alert, not accusation. Every output carries the fixed notice: "Statistical signal based on public financial statements. Not an accusation, not investment advice."
- No public ranking: no list of companies ranked by alert, percentile or score, in any output (documents, API and interface).
- Per-company results stay out of git (`resultados/`); the ground truth only has public cases.
- No personal data: no CPF and no natural person's name (an individual auditor appears without a name).
