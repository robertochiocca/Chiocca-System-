# Project history

What left the README on 2026-11-21: the history of the phases, from study 1 to study 2's sealed list, and the table of what is done and what is on the roadmap. The current state, in 30 seconds, is in the [README](../../README.en.md).

## Study 1 (2010 to 2023)

**First-version study complete.** The one-page summary is in [`docs/summary.md`](../summary.md), and the full figures, including the ground truth case by case, the naive baseline rule side by side, the secondary analyses and the robustness test, are in [`docs/en/results.md`](results.md). Any rule change or new study depends on authorization.

The ground truth ([`data/gabarito_casos.csv`](../../data/gabarito_casos.csv)) holds real cases, each with the affected period, the date it became public, the source and whether the case is detectable in the main analysis. Amendments 6 to 9 of the pre-registration expanded it with CVM sanctioning proceedings; cases without a final decision are kept in [`data/casos_em_observacao.csv`](../../data/casos_em_observacao.csv). The two starting cases:

- **Americanas (2023):** revealed from the inside. The Independent Committee confirmed fictitious cooperative advertising allowances and supplier-finance operations booked as trade payables ([material fact notice of 2024-07-16](https://api.mziq.com/mzfilemanager/v2/d/347dba24-05d2-479e-a775-2ea8677c50f2/fbd1eb14-1e3f-f8ef-ecc5-f49db62d1b04?origin=1), in Portuguese).
- **IRB Brasil (2020):** revealed from the outside, by an asset manager's thesis that recurring profit was lower than reported profit ([InfoMoney](https://www.infomoney.com.br/?p=1397142), in Portuguese). As a reinsurer, it is excluded from the M-score, which does not apply to financial companies, and is assessed by the share of non-recurring gains.

## Product v2

**Chiocca System v2, from study to product (in progress).** Study 1 is frozen on the `estudo-1-final` branch, and the product uses engine v1 as it is. Phase A complete: inventory of the code from Carchuna, Calahonda and DireitoAberto ([`docs/en/inventory.md`](inventory.md)), data contracts ([`docs/en/contracts.md`](contracts.md)), plan for phases B to F ([`docs/en/plan-v2.md`](plan-v2.md)) and the seal on fiscal years 2024 onward (`chiocca/v2/lacre.py`), reserved for study 2. Phase B complete: validity, CVM data checks, lineage and confidence ([`docs/en/trust-engine.md`](trust-engine.md)), with the result of the checks on the whole base in [`docs/en/data-checks.md`](data-checks.md), and CI on GitHub Actions. Phase C1 complete: temporal storage (DuckDB over Parquet), engine v1 on any reference date and the study 1 invariant ([`docs/en/temporal-storage.md`](temporal-storage.md)). Phase C2 complete: original scale, contract evidence, ENET fetching through a separate command, context (percentiles, history, Altman Z'' and Piotroski F, with no alert), auditor's report and auditor, the "Limits" section and the investigation in JSON and PDF, with two Americanas examples (fiscal year 2021, on 2022-04-30 and on 2024-12-31) in [`docs/exemplos/`](../exemplos/). Phase D complete: FastAPI API (`/api/v1`), with Pydantic contracts, search only by name, CVM code or CNPJ, in alphabetical order, and no list of companies ranked by alert, percentile or score ([`docs/en/contracts.md`](contracts.md), section 6). Phase E complete: interface in `web/` (Next.js), in PT and EN, with dark mode and mobile, the fixed notice on every screen, aggregate-only sector comparison and the "How the engine was tested" page ([`docs/en/contracts.md`](contracts.md), section 7); demo mode turned on by configuration. Static demo prepared: the demo-mode investigations generated by a script, in JSON and PDF, so the interface can run as a static site, with no API (section 7 of the contracts). Phase F1 in progress: extractive reader of the explanatory notes, the auditor's report and the Reference Form, only with copied and literally checked excerpts, with no alert, with the measurement pre-registered before the code ([`docs/en/reader-preregistration.md`](reader-preregistration.md)); the "Document excerpts" section was switched on on 2026-11-11, after the frozen reader met the four targets on the 20 held-out cases ([`docs/en/contracts.md`](contracts.md), section 8). Phase F2a in progress: material facts and market notices filed by the date, with IPE metadata only, in "What was known on the date", and the CVM monitor, which only lists the novelties for review ([`docs/en/contracts.md`](contracts.md), section 9). Phase F2b: under amendment 1 to the reader's pre-registration, the second reader version (`leitor-f1-2`) and a subject-specific rule for the material facts were frozen and measured once on new held-out cases; both passed, and since 2026-11-13 the excerpts section uses `leitor-f1-2` and the list uses the new rule ([`docs/en/contracts.md`](contracts.md), sections 8 and 9). The static demo has been published on GitHub Pages since 2026-11-10; the deploy with a live API was postponed (Roberto's decision of 2026-11-15, [`plan-v2.md`](plan-v2.md)).
