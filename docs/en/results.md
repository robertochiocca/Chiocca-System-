## Rules applied

- **Point-in-time version:** each company-year as of its cut-off date, from the Phase 1 base; t comes from the ÚLTIMO (current year) column and t−1 from the PENÚLTIMO (prior year) column of the same DFP (amendment 5).
- **Accounts:** by the codes confirmed in Phase 0 (reconnaissance, part 1.4). Depreciation and interest paid by the frozen text patterns (reconnaissance, part 4.2), with the parent-account rule and the text comparison of amendment 7.
- **Interest paid:** amounts in operating cash flow (section 6.01) are moved to financing for all companies; the adjusted operating cash flow enters TATA and accruals (pre-registration, section 4.1).
- **Depreciation and amortization in a single line:** the total is used and the company-year is flagged (section 4.1).
- **Missing account, zero denominator or negative revenue in the denominator:** not computable (section 4.1 and amendment 10). No neutral values and no winsorizing (section 4.1).
- **CDI rate:** Banco Central do Brasil SGS series 4389 (docs/en/sources.md); average CDI of the year = average of the daily rates over the 12 months ending on the DFP reference date.
- **Alerts (section 4):** M > -1.78; DPO change in the top decile of the year among non-financial companies; implied interest rate > average CDI + 10 p.p.; non-recurring weight in the top decile of the year among financial companies. A non-financial company is in alert when at least one of the three signals fires; accruals do not trigger alerts.
- **Naive baseline rule (amendment 7):** (total assets − top-level shareholders' equity) ÷ total assets, top decile of each year within each group.
- **Detection (section 5):** an alert in at least one fiscal year of the affected period, between 2010 and 2023, with the point-in-time version filed before the public date. In all those fiscal years the cut-off date is also before the public date (0 exceptions).

## Implementation choices in this phase

The pre-registration does not go down to these details. Each was fixed before running the sample, with no alternative tested. Amendment 11 accepted the choices as recorded; the direct-method cash flow statement with no depreciation line stays as a limitation.

- **Top decile:** a value greater than or equal to the 90th percentile of the computable values of the fiscal year and group (percentile with linear interpolation, the pandas default).
- **Evaluated company:** at least one of the group's alert signals is computable. The alert rate is companies in alert ÷ evaluated companies; the sample size is shown alongside.
- **Direct-method cash flow statement:** the text patterns were applied to whichever cash flow statement the company files. In the 190 company-years with a direct-method statement there is no depreciation line, and the M-score is not computable (missing account).
- **Non-financial companies on another chart of accounts:** the Phase 0 codes apply to the non-financial chart. In the 15 non-financial company-years on the bank chart or an unrecognized one, the M-score, DPO and implied interest rate are not computable (reason: chart of accounts).
- **Interest paid not found:** operating cash flow stays as reported. Lines found in section 6.01 (moved) in 2,716 company-years and in section 6.03 (already in financing) in 1,929.
- **Cases under observation in the false-alarm count:** the whole company is left out, in every year (Oi has a case under observation for its 2015 and 2016 statements and also a ground-truth case, Oi 2014, in the secondary analysis).
- **Amendment 3:** applied to the 20 ground-truth cases outside the secondary analysis that have a CVM code and a public date; the last DFP is the most recent company-year in the sample filed before that date.
- **CPC 06 (R2) flag:** fiscal years starting in 2019 (498 company-years). The flag changes no calculation.

## Explanation after the result, not a test

Descriptive analysis done after seeing the results. It changes no rule, cut-off or case, and it does not count as a test: the questions and comparisons were chosen knowing the result.

**(a) Americanas' SGI and DSRI in 2021.**

- Source: minutes of the B2W extraordinary shareholders' meeting (CVM code 20990, later Americanas S.A.) of 2021-06-10, which approved the partial spin-off of Lojas Americanas (CVM code 8087), with all its operating assets and related liabilities transferred to B2W at their book value as of 2020-12-31; the material fact notice of 2021-04-28 says that equity changes from that base date also go to B2W. Documents and hashes in docs/en/sources.md.
- SGI: the 2020 comparative in the 2021 DFP is B2W alone, with net revenue of R$10,124 million; 2021 net revenue is R$22,696 million. Lojas Americanas on a consolidated basis, which already included B2W, had net revenue of R$21,291 million in 2020. The SGI of 2.24 comes from this change of perimeter.
- DSRI: trade receivables ÷ net revenue was 0.31 in 2021, 0.18 at B2W in 2020 and 0.16 at Lojas Americanas in 2020. The DSRI of 1.70 is not explained by the combination alone, because the 2021 ratio is above both.
- Replacing t−1 with the 2020 consolidated DFP of Lojas Americanas (perimeters not identical): SGI 1.07, DSRI 1.96, AQI 1.71 and M = -0.96, alert. Without the SGI effect, M would still be above -1.78, driven by DSRI and AQI.
