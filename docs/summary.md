# Summary · Chiocca System

Generated by `python -m chiocca.relatorio_resultados` from `docs/resultados_numeros.json`. Details, tables and sources in [`docs/en/results.md`](en/results.md). Statistical alert based on public financial statements. Not an accusation, not investment advice.

**Question.**
Using public data available at the time, would the catalog signals have flagged the ground-truth cases before they became public? How many false alarms would that cost?

**Design.**

- Pre-registration signed on 2026-11-04, with 11 amendments; only the last one was written after the results, and it changes only the false-alarm metric.
- All listed companies filing DFPs with the CVM, 2010 to 2023, including delisted ones: 6,938 company-years of non-financial companies with revenue and 517 of financial companies, from 987 companies.
- Point-in-time: each company-year uses the DFP version that was public on the cut-off date (last day of the fourth month after fiscal year-end); versions refiled later come from the CVM's ENET system.
- Non-financial companies: M-score (alert if M > -1.78), DPO jump (top decile of the year) and implied interest rate (above the average CDI rate + 10 p.p.). Financial companies: weight of non-recurring gains (top decile of the year).
- Ground truth: 42 cases, of which 13 are detectable in the main study. Test period, 2017 to 2023, opened once, on 2026-11-07.

**Result.**

- Detection: 7 of 13 detectable cases (7 of 42 in the whole ground truth).
- False alarms per detected case among non-financial companies (amendment 11): 311.0 in the test period, with 2 detections by an alert within it, and 108.3 over the whole period. Alert rate: 49.9% of evaluated company-years.
- Financial companies: no detectable case. IRB had no alert in its 2018 DFP (0.053, against a threshold of 1.024).

**What went wrong and why.**

- The implied interest rate alone accounts for 59.8% of company-years in alert (2,001 of 3,345). Finance costs (3.06.02) include, by definition, exchange differences, lease interest and other items that are not interest on borrowings (2.01.04 and 2.02.01); this breakdown was not measured. Without it, the alert rate would be 20.0% instead of 49.9%.
- The M-score had no value in 1,548 of 6,938 company-years; in 1,306, DEPI was missing. In 7 of the 13 detectable cases M did not exist because the cash flow statement has no depreciation line (2 of them with a direct-method statement). With neutral values, detection would be 8 of 13.
- Only 2 of the 7 detections have an alert in the test period: in 11 of the 13 detectable cases, the affected period ends before 2017.
- Amendment 10 put the numerator and the detections in different windows (88.1 false alarms per detected case); amendment 11 fixed this after the results, and both versions are in the report.

**Pre-registered predictions (section 4.2).**

- Right: Americanas' M-score fired before 2023, with M = -0.24 in 2021; the DPO, at -6.8 days, and the implied interest rate, at 9.4 p.p. above CDI, did not fire; GMI was 0.95, below 1; IRB had no alert before the public date.
- Wrong: Americanas' M was not driven by TATA and AQI, with contributions of 0.14 and 0.49, but by SGI and DSRI, with 2.00 and 1.57, the two largest contributions.

**Americanas: the 2021 alert comes partly from the combination.**

In 2021, B2W (CVM code 20990) received the Lojas Americanas operations through a partial spin-off (shareholders' meeting of 2021-06-10). The 2020 comparative is B2W alone: net revenue went from R$10,124 million to R$22,696 million, and the SGI of 2.24 comes from this change of perimeter. With Lojas Americanas' 2020 consolidated statements as the base, SGI falls to 1.07 (a contribution of 0.95, against 2.00), and M stays above the cut-off, at -0.96, driven by DSRI and AQI, with indices of 1.96 and 1.71. In the restated reading from the 2022 DFP, M is also above the cut-off, at -0.90. This explanation was made after the result and is not a test.

**Limitations.**

- Few cases: 13 detectable out of 42 in the ground truth, none of them financial; the main-study ceiling is 13 of 42.
- IRB cannot be detected with annual DFPs (amendment 3); catching it would require quarterly data (ITR).
- Signals calibrated in the US: the -1.78 cut-off comes from Beneish (1999) and was not recalibrated for Brazil.
- Finance costs (3.06.02) that do not separate interest on borrowings: the implied interest rate fires in 40.7% of the company-years where it is computable.
- Direct-method cash flow statements have no depreciation line in 190 company-years, leaving M not computable (a limitation accepted in amendment 11).
- The Americanas case became public from the inside, through the company's own material fact notice of 2023-01-11, not through a signal in the financial statements.
- Amendment 11 was written after seeing the results; the earlier version of the metric remains in the report.
